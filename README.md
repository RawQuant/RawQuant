# RawQuant

**Daily stock signals from a machine learning model.** Website: [rawquant.com](https://www.rawquant.com/)

RawQuant publishes the portfolio of a machine learning stock model every US trading day at about 3:45 PM New York time, 15 minutes before the close. Every signal is timestamped and kept on the record, losses included.

## How it works

- The model picks US stocks and sets a target weight for each one.
- Each position is held for 12 trading days. The book runs 36 overlapping slices, so part of it turns over every day.
- You place the orders yourself, in any broker. RawQuant never connects to your account and never trades for you.
- The record through 2025 is a backtest and is labelled that way everywhere. The rules were locked before 2026 began, so the 2026 record is a test nothing was tuned to pass.
